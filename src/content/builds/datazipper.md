Stock prices against weather. News mention volume against anything. Your own CSV against all of it. Datazipper aligns mismatched time series on a shared axis and picks a visualization that fits the shape of the data.

## Why it exists

Most "compare two datasets" tasks die in the alignment step: different granularities, time zones, missing days. Datazipper does that part once so you can get to the interesting question.
